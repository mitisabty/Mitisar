OUR ESSENCE

An Escape Worth Savoring

Tucked away along the lively corridor of GS Road, Uptown Escape was created as an intimate sanctuary from the city's frantic pace. We bring together artisanal cafe culture by day and a sultry, sophisticated cocktail lounge by dusk.

From sunlit espresso brunches and handmade Italian mains to late-night craft mixology under botanical foliage, every moment is handcrafted for comfort, luxury, and warmth.
